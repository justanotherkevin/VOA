How it works

1

Hit Cmd+Shift+Space from any app

VOA starts listening in any context. Zoom, your browser, the terminal. The same shortcut starts and stops recording.

2

Speak normally

VOA notices when you pause and transcribes what you've said as you go, so there's no waiting once the meeting ends.

3

Get your transcript

Your transcript and summary are both saved right on your Mac, ready whenever you need them.

What's under the hood

Production-grade local models. No cloud shortcuts.

keyboard_command_key

One key, from anywhere

Cmd+Shift+Space starts and stops recording regardless of which app is focused. Works in Zoom, Google Meet, your browser, or a terminal window.

Cmd + Shift + Space to record
memory

Runs on your machine

Whisper AI handles transcription, and a summarization model built into the app writes the summary. Both run on your machine and never send a byte outside your Mac.

leak_add

Auto-labels your meetings

VOA reads macOS Accessibility APIs to detect when you're in Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, or Slack and names the recording for you.

auto_awesome

Summaries, not just transcripts

Reads your transcript and pulls out what mattered: decisions made, tasks assigned, and topics that took the most time.

  • check_circle Key Decisions
  • check_circle Action Items
  • check_circle Topic Breakdown
keyboard_voice

Dictate into any app

A separate shortcut turns on dictation mode: VOA types what you say straight into whatever window you're working in, no meeting required.

Zero Data Exposure

Your audio is transcribed as you speak and never saved to a file. Only the text is kept, right on your own Mac.

Your audio never leaves your Mac.

Most AI tools route your audio through data centers you don't control. VOA runs the models locally. No API keys. No fine print. Full offline functionality once you download the models.
