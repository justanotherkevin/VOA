# VOA Mission

Draft, not approved. Living doc, expected to change as the product does.

## Purpose

VOA exists to own the full audio-to-action loop, on desktop and mobile, without
handing your audio to anyone else's server.

## Scope

- Platforms: desktop (current) and mobile (planned).
- Everything in scope: audio. Meetings, dictation, notes, calls, voice memos.
- Everything out of scope until it touches audio: unrelated productivity
  features (calendars, chat, general note taking) that don't originate from
  or feed back into audio.

## The audio loop

1. **Capture** — record audio from a meeting, call, or dictation, on whichever
   device you're using.
2. **Transcribe** — turn audio into text, on-device, in real time or near
   real time.
3. **Summarize** — turn a transcript into meeting notes: what was discussed,
   what was decided.
4. **Extract** — pull action items and commitments out of the notes.
5. **Remind** — surface those action items back to the user at the right
   time, so nothing said out loud gets lost.

## Non-negotiables

- Audio and transcripts stay on-device by default. Cloud is opt-in, never
  required.
- No account required to use the core loop.
- Desktop and mobile share the same audio-to-action pipeline, not two
  separate products that happen to share a name.
